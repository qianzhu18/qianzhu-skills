# AGENTS

This repo contains personal content-production skills for qianzhu / Lucien.

When working in this repo, optimize for two things at once:

1. keep the writing unmistakably like qianzhu
2. keep the content distributable in public feeds

That means the agent should not stop at “正文写得像本人”.
It should also check whether the content can win the click before the正文 starts.

## Core Principle

For qianzhu-style personal-brand writing:

- the article body carries the judgment
- the title carries the click reason
- the cover carries the work's visual identity

Do not treat title and cover as cosmetic afterthoughts.
Treat them as part of the content system.

## Default Planning Rules

When a task involves公众号长文, X article, title optimization, cover optimization, or content packaging, plan in this order:

1. clarify the content's core judgment
2. identify the target reader and why they should care now
3. draft the body structure
4. draft the title as a hook, not a summary
5. draft the cover as a work-facing visual, not a decorative image
6. draft short distribution copy if needed
7. verify that title, cover, and opening paragraph are aligned

Do not jump straight from body draft to final delivery without checking the entrance layer.

## Title Rules

Titles should usually do at least two of the following:

1. name a concrete object
2. contain tension, contrast, or a surprising claim
3. show cost, time, trial, or real involvement
4. deliver a judgment, not just a topic

Prefer titles that feel like:

- a tested conclusion
- a specific discovery
- a strong question with built-in tension

Avoid titles that feel like:

- article summaries
- essay topics
- “关于……的思考”
- “最近对……的理解”
- generic reflection labels

Short rule:

`标题是钩子，不是总结。`

## Cover Rules

Covers should make the piece feel like a finished work.

For qianzhu's long-form content, prefer:

- black-and-white or low-saturation direction when suitable
- strong typography
- minimal decoration
- high contrast
- one dominant judgment on the cover

The cover's job is not to repeat the full article title.
Its job is to compress the hardest-hitting idea into a more visual first impression.

Short rule:

`封面不是配图，而是作品门面。`

## Growth Calibration

If a post underperforms, do not assume the body is the only problem.
Check the entrance stack first:

1. title hook
2. cover recognizability
3. account positioning signal
4. topic-reader fit
5. opening paragraph handoff

Do not reduce every growth issue to “标题不行”.
But do not ignore title/cover problems either.

## When Updating Skills

If you update `qianzhu-writing-style`, keep these layers connected:

- voice and persona
- methodology and structure
- title and cover conversion
- fullchain delivery expectations

If a new rule changes how content should be distributed, reflected, packaged, or visually framed, update both:

1. the relevant reference file
2. the higher-level instructions in `SKILL.md` when needed

If the change is repo-wide behavior rather than single-skill detail, update this `AGENTS.md`.

## Minimum Review Before Finishing

Before calling the work done, check:

- Is the writing still clearly qianzhu?
- Is the title a hook rather than a summary?
- Does the cover create work identity instead of just filling space?
- Does the first screen of the article deserve the title's promise?
- If the task is fullchain, are the packaging assets actually complete?
